Our Kitchen

Food that feels
like home.

At Chaitrali's Kitchen, every dish is prepared with the warmth of home cooking and the attention to detail your special occasions deserve. Born from a love of authentic Marathi cuisine, each meal carries the flavors of Maharashtra — from varan-bhaat to puran poli.

We cook with no shortcuts, no artificial preservatives, and absolutely no meat or eggs — ever. Just pure vegetarian food the way it was meant to taste.

Based in Quincy, MA, we serve the greater South Shore — bringing the warmth of an Indian kitchen to your table every week.

— Chaitrali
