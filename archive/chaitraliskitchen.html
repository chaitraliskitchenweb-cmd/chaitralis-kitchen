Our Story

Cooking is Chaitra's love language

"Food is the thread that connects us to our roots."

Born and raised in India, Chaitra has been cooking since she was old enough to stand at the stove. Her kitchen is where old recipes meet a new home — where the scent of cardamom, turmeric, and ghee tells the story of generations.

Every meal is prepared to order, by hand, with ingredients sourced fresh. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just real Indian food, the way it was always meant to taste.

For the Owner

Your Menu Lives in Google Sheets

Set up your Google Sheet with the columns below and this website updates automatically — no coding needed.

Google Sheet Column Headers (Row 1) 
# ── Required Columns ──────────────────────
Name         | Dish name  (e.g. "Butter Chicken")
Category     | Starters / Mains / Breads / Desserts / Drinks
Price        | Number only  (e.g. 12.99)
Description  | Short 1–2 line description
Available    | TRUE or FALSE

# ── Optional Columns ──────────────────────
Emoji        | 🍛  (one emoji to represent the dish)
Veg          | TRUE or FALSE
Spicy        | TRUE or FALSE
IsNew        | TRUE or FALSE

# ── Reviews Sheet (tab name: "Reviews") ───
Name  Location  Rating  Review

📤 Publishing Your Sheet

1. Open your Google Sheet → File → Share → Publish to web
2. Choose Comma-separated values (.csv) for each sheet tab
3. Click Publish and copy the URL
4. Click Configure Sheet above and paste your URL — done! ✅
